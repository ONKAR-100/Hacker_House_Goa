# 🌴⚡ HH GOA 2026 — FRAME IN GOA

## **BUILD. VIBE. FRAME. GOA.**


A fun, interactive **HH Goa 2026 Photo Frame & ID Card Generator** that lets builders create personalized Hacker House Goa identities, generate a unique team frame with their crew, download their creations, and share them on X.

### `#FrameInGoa` 🌊

---

## ✦ What is **Frame in Goa**?

**Frame in Goa** is a lightweight, browser-based photo frame generator created around the **Hacker House Goa 2026** experience.

Upload your photo, add your name and tech stack, get your personalized **Builder Class**, and generate a shareable HH Goa 2026 frame — all within seconds.

You can also bring your teammates together into a completely new **Team Pass / Crew Card** featuring everyone's photographs and a generated team identity.

> **One photo. One stack. One Builder Class. One frame.**

---

# ✨ Features

## 🪪 Personal HH Goa Frame

Create your own personalized Hacker House Goa identity.

* 📸 Upload any photo
* ✂️ **No manual cropping required**
* ✍️ Add your name

## 👥 Team / Crew Frame

### **One builder is cool. A whole crew is better.** 🌴

Create a completely new **Team Pass / Crew Card** instead of simply combining individual ID cards.

The Team Frame includes:

* 🏷️ Custom team name
* 📸 **2–4 team photographs**
* 👤 Member names
* 🔑 **ACCESS: BUILDER**
* 🪪 Generated **Pass ID**
* ⚡ Generated team-level **Builder Title**
* 🌴 HH Goa 2026 branding
* `#FrameInGoa`
* 🌊 Subtle Goa-inspired doodles
* 🖼️ Horizontal/landscape layout optimized for multiple photographs
* ⬇️ Downloadable team image
* 𝕏 Share-to-X functionality

The result is designed as **one cohesive collectible team pass**, not a box containing separate ID cards.

# 🔒 Privacy

Photos are processed primarily within the user's browser.

The application does not require users to create an account or upload their photographs to a dedicated image-storage service simply to generate a frame.

**Personal photographs should not be stored or transmitted unnecessarily.**
